The first four houses match exactly. The tens place is the first place they differ — 1 versus 5 — and that decides everything.

Clue #2 · The Why

Why not start at the ones?

Scan from the biggest house. Stop at the first difference:

The magnifying glass scans left to right — biggest house first

Once an earlier (bigger) house is different, nothing smaller can ever change the outcome. If the thousands are already different, no digit in the hundreds, tens, or ones could ever flip who's greater — those smaller houses just don't have enough value to catch up.

Explain in your own words

How do you find the first place where two numbers differ?

Model answer: Line up the digits by house, starting from the biggest place value. Move across, comparing one house at a time. Stop as soon as the digits don't match — that house decides which number is greater.
