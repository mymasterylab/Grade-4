The 6 in 36,241 and the 2 in 12,315 are combined — why?

Both digits sit in the thousands place: 6 thousands + 2 thousands = 8 thousands. They're combined because they're the same place-value unit — not because they look alike or sit in the middle.

The intended addend was 6,400, but the record shows:
