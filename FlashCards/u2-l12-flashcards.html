Clue #1

One calculation. Three meanings.

The number sentence 52 − 27 = 25 can describe three completely different real situations:

🪙 Take AwayWhat remains after removing?
📏 DifferenceHow far apart are they?
❓ Missing AddendWhat is needed to complete?
The answer is always 25 — but what the subtraction means depends on the story around it.
Mission A — Take Away

🪙 What remains?

The explorer starts with 52 coins and gives 27 to the village.

📏 How far apart?

The village has 27 coins. The explorer has 52. How far apart are those two amounts?

Difference52 − 27 = 25

We measure the gap between two amounts. Neither is being removed — we're comparing.

Mission C — Missing Addend

❓ What is missing?

The village has 27 coins. The explorer needs a total of 52. How many more are needed?

Bar model
52 (target)
27 (already have)
? still needed
Missing Addend27 + ? = 52   ⇒   ? = 25

We know a part and the whole — subtraction finds the missing part.

Clue #2 — The Why

The Treasure Fact Family

One addition fact always creates two subtraction facts:

27 + 25 =52

52 − 25 =27
The Connection

Addition checks subtraction

Addition and subtraction are inverse operations — they undo each other. Use this to check any subtraction answer:

Subtraction73 − 28 = 45
Check with addition45 + 28 = 73 ✓

If the addition gets back to the minuend (73), the subtraction is correct.

The whole is made of two parts. If you know the whole and one part, subtraction finds the other part. Adding the two parts must give back the whole — always.

Explain in your own words

Why can addition be used to check subtraction?

Model answer: addition and subtraction are inverse operations — they undo each other. The whole is made of two parts, so if subtraction separates them, adding them back must return the whole. If the addition equals the minuend, the subtraction was correct.
