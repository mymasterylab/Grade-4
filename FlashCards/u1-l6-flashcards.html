Clue #1

About how many books?

A school has 2,487 books in one section and 3,612 in another. Without calculating exactly — about how many in total?

Estimate first2,500 + 3,600 ≈ 6,100
Then calculate exactly2,487 + 3,612 = 6,099

The estimate (6,100) landed just 1 away from the exact answer (6,099) — close enough to trust the calculation, and fast enough to do in your head.

Field Test #1

Estimate the total seats

Round to the nearest thousand18,000 + 22,000 ≈ 40,000
