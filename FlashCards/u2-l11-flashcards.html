Clue #2 · The Why

Not "carry the 1" — regroup it

Instead of saying "carry the 1," say what's really happening:

No! The regrouped 1 is never just "one." In the tens column, it's worth 1 ten (10). In the hundreds column, it's worth 1 hundred (100). Its value always depends on which column it lands in.
