Clue #1 · The Pattern

What connects these numbers?

5,000 → 50,000 → 500,000

5,000 × 10= 50,000
50,000 × 10= 500,000

Continue it

500,000 × 10= 5,000,000

Now reverse it (÷10 each time)

500,000 ÷ 10= 50,000
50,000 ÷ 10= 5,000
5,000 ÷ 10= 500

Every step is the same relationship — ×10 going right, ÷10 going left. That's place value in action.

Suspect Statement

Find the counterexample

A learner says:

"If two numbers have the same number of digits, the one with the larger last digit is greater."
41>39

39 has the bigger last digit (9), but 41 is still the greater number. One counterexample is all it takes to prove a claim false.

Explain the relationship between 6,000 and 60,000.

Create two numbers that round to the same 10,000.
