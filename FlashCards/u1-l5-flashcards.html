Clue #2 · The Why

Forget "look at the digit"

Rounding really means: which benchmark is nearest?

closer to lower benchmark → round DOWN
closer to upper benchmark → round UP
exactly halfway: convention says round UP
lower benchmark
upper benchmark

Once you know which side of halfway a number sits on, you don't need to measure the exact distance every time. That's why we can just glance at the next digit — it's a shortcut for something deeper: finding the nearer benchmark.

Explain in your own words

Why does your answer change at the halfway point?

Model answer: at the exact halfway point, a number is equally close to both benchmarks — "nearest" can't decide for us anymore. So we all agree on one rule, the rounding convention, and send exact ties up.
